# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui (`base-nova`), motion. One site in this directory. Deploy target stated in the brief: GitHub repository `physiovita-koeln` and Vercel production.

## Users

Inferred: people in and around Köln who need a physiotherapy practice, often with an ärztliche Verordnung, looking for the practice, its treatments, how to get there, and a way to make an appointment. The brief does not name a narrower audience.

## Product Purpose

A one-page public website for the physiotherapy practice Physiovita Köln. Success is a visitor who understands what the practice is, where it is, and can call for a Termin.

## Positioning

Physiovita Köln is a physiotherapy practice at Olpener Straße 925, 51109 Köln, led by the owners named in the existing imprint. The site replaces https://physiovita-koeln.de/ as the public face. It does not claim outcomes, rankings, or a booking product the practice does not operate.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search or a referral, read on a phone or a desktop, and call. Appointments are by telephone. The existing site also publishes an email address and a fax number. Opening hours on the contact page and the homepage agree; the team page states a wider Friday window (recorded under Evidence, not used as the displayed hours).

## Product Principles

- Say only what the existing practice has already published, or mark it as inferred.
- The telephone appointment is the action, visible without scrolling on a typical phone.
- Clinical calm over promotion. No celebrity wall, no invented relief claims.
- Location and hours are as easy to find as the treatment list.
- Legal pages name the known responsible parties and stay explicitly incomplete where a lawyer still has to review the new host.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred: visitors include people in pain, older adults, and anyone on a phone in daylight. Text contrast, a visible phone action, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support are required. No further accessibility standard was specified.
